# 0912-003 Product descriptions — Blunts and Pre-Rolls

Date: 2026-09-12
Scope: /blunts and /pre-rolls. PAULIEWOOD requested replacing all artwork commentary with product descriptions and publishing the result.

## Evidence rules applied

- Composition comes from the current supplied packages, supported by the existing 6170 packaging transcription. The September drop takes precedence over older menu labels.
- Brand product listings establish the infusion method and collaboration. Weedmaps strain/flavor modules are reported cultivar context, not batch lab results or proof of identical genetics across all markets.
- Known cultivar notes are framed as profile descriptions. No effect, medical, potency-percentage, dose, safety, or guaranteed burn-time claims were carried from the listings.
- Label variants matter: Pink Cookies and Gorilla Goo shown here include live resin, distillate, and diamonds; the Skywalker pre-roll shown here says distillate and kief despite an older menu transcription listing live resin and diamonds.
- The pictured self-titled PRESIDENTIAL product is now named Presidential (Classic), avoiding conflation with the separate Presidential OG live-resin product.
- Ghost Haze Train was corrected to the printed product name Ghost Train Haze.
- Cereal Milk and Wedding Cake recipes come from the supplied labels (flower, live rosin, diamonds); flavor notes are cultivar context, not a claim that an independent Presidential batch was tasted.
- Cosmic Cookies has ambiguous cultivar references and no corroborated exact Presidential flavor description. Copy describes the labeled hybrid/live-rosin recipe instead of inventing flavors.
- Apricotti's Moxie live-resin/kief formulation is confirmed; specific apricot tasting notes were not found and were not invented.
- Fruit-named flavor products use their declared flavor identity, without inventing fruit ingredients or using artwork as evidence of extra flavors.
- Images, layout, product ordering, and existing scroll-reset behavior remain as before. All 63 displayed descriptions (36 blunt entries, 27 pre-roll entries) changed; existing alternate pre-roll images retain matching product copy.
